These icon assets are Material Design Icons (MDI), the icon set used by Home
Assistant. They are by Pictogrammers and licensed under Apache-2.0. The SVG
sources and their 24x24 monochrome BMP renderings are kept together here. BMPs
are rasterized from the SVGs with a white background for ESPHome's binary-image
component.
